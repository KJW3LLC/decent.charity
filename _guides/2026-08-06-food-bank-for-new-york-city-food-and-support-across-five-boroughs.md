**Food Bank For New York City: Food and Support Across Five Boroughs**
==================================================

Food Bank For New York City works to end hunger across the city’s five boroughs by providing food, SNAP assistance, nutrition education, and disaster relief. As a member of Feeding America, it partners with local agencies, corporations, foundations, and community groups to distribute resources and support New Yorkers facing food insecurity.

## Organization Background
The organization originated as New York City’s first food bank and has grown to become the city’s largest hunger relief organization. Its mission is to empower every New Yorker to achieve food security—providing fresh food, SNAP assistance, and nutrition education for lasting stability. The vision is “Feeding today—fueling a food‑secure future.” Food Bank For NYC operates as a local affiliate of Feeding America, collaborating with member agencies, corporate and foundation partners, and community organizations to deliver food and services throughout the boroughs.

## Communities Served
Food Bank For NYC serves individuals and families across all five boroughs, including students, seniors, and working households. It provides access to free groceries and hot meals through a network of about 800 locations, community kitchens, mobile pantries, and campus pantries. Services also include SNAP enrollment help, tax filing assistance, nutrition education, financial empowerment programs, and disaster relief when needed. All services are offered with dignity to New Yorkers experiencing food insecurity.

## Programs and Services
The organization’s principal work includes:
- **Food Distribution**: serving every NYC borough with dignity through pantries, kitchens, mobile distributions, and campus pantries.
- **Financial Empowerment**: helping New Yorkers stretch their dollars through benefits counseling and related support.
- **Nutrition Education**: teaching healthy eating habits to promote long‑term well‑being.
- **Disaster Relief**: showing up when the city faces emergencies to provide food and essential supplies.
- **SNAP Assistance** and **Tax Filing Help**: connecting eligible residents to federal benefits and refunds.

These services are local to New York City; Food Bank For NYC is a member of the national Feeding America network but delivers its programs specifically within the city’s five boroughs.
