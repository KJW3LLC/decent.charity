**One Warm Coat: Sharing Warmth Through Community Coat Drives**
============================================================

One Warm Coat is a national nonprofit that provides free coats to children and adults in need while encouraging volunteerism and promoting environmental sustainability. The organization works through a network of local partners to collect and distribute coats, giving outerwear a second life and reducing textile waste. For more information, visit the [One Warm Coat official website](https://www.onewarmcoat.org/about-us/).

## Organization Background
Founded in 1992 by one woman with a single coat to give, One Warm Coat has grown into a national movement rooted in humanitarianism, volunteerism, and environmental stewardship. Its mission is to provide free coats to people in need, foster community involvement, and promote sustainability through the reuse of outerwear. The organization operates as a nonprofit, relying on volunteers, donors, and partnerships with local groups to carry out its work.

## Communities Served
One Warm Coat serves children and adults who lack adequate outerwear across the United States. By partnering with more than 1,500 local nonprofit organizations in all 50 states, the organization reaches individuals and families experiencing cold‑weather hardship. Coats are provided free of charge, without discrimination or obligation, and recipients are treated with dignity and respect.

## Programs and Services
The organization’s principal work centers on two interconnected programs:

- **Coat Drive program**: Collects new and gently used coats from the public and redistributes them through local nonprofit partners, keeping garments out of landfills and giving outerwear a second life.
- **Zero Waste Initiative**: Collaborates with manufacturers and retailers to accept and distribute overstock, irregular, and sample outerwear that cannot be sold, further reducing textile waste.

While the organization’s impact is national—spanning all 50 states—the actual collection and distribution of coats occur locally through its partner nonprofits.
