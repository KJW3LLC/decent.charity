**Feeding America: A Nationwide Network Working to End Hunger**
============================================================

Feeding America is the largest hunger‑relief organization in the United States, working to end hunger through a nationwide network of food banks and partner agencies. Its mission is to advance change by ensuring fair access to nutritious food for all, in partnership with food banks, policymakers, supporters, and the communities served. Learn more at [Feeding America official website](https://www.feedingamerica.org/about-us).

## Organization Background
Feeding America was founded as a nationwide network that connects people to food and builds long‑term solutions to hunger. It operates as the largest hunger‑relief organization in the U.S., with a mission to ensure fair access to nutritious food for everyone. The organization’s work includes food distribution, policy and advocacy, research and data, and supporting good health through reliable access to fresh and preferred foods.

## Communities Served
Through its network, Feeding America serves people across all 50 states and Puerto Rico. The network comprises more than 250 food banks, over 20 statewide food bank associations, more than 10 regional cooperatives, and upwards of 60,000 agency partners such as local food pantries and meal programs. Together they provide approximately 5.9 billion meals each year to individuals and families facing hunger.

## Programs and Services
Feeding America’s core work is carried out by its national network, while direct service delivery happens locally through its partner agencies. National‑level activities include:
- Moving food quickly, safely and reliably to tens of thousands of local pantries and meal programs.
- Advocating with lawmakers to strengthen programs like SNAP and address hunger’s root causes.
- Conducting research and publishing data such as Map the Meal Gap to inform policy and community action.
- Supporting initiatives that increase access to nutritious, fresh and preferred foods for health needs.
Local services, provided by the network’s agency partners, include mobile food pantries, drive‑thru food pantries, kids’ programs, disaster response, food rescue, and assistance with government programs such as SNAP, WIC, and school meals.

- **Needed Goods**: Donate food, host a food drive, or contribute to food rescue efforts.
