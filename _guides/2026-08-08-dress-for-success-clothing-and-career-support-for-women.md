**Dress for Success: Clothing and Career Support for Women**
====================================================================

Dress for Success Worldwide empowers women to achieve economic mobility by providing a global network of support, workplace attire, and development tools for success. Founded in 1996, the organization aims to create a world where women thrive in work and in life through coaching, clothing, and a continuing network of assistance.

## Organization Background
Dress for Success began in 1996 with the vision of helping women thrive professionally and personally. Its mission is to advance women in the workplace by offering a global network of support, professional clothing, and career development resources. Today, the organization operates as a network of 130 affiliates in 15 countries, having assisted more than 1.3 million women worldwide.

## Communities Served
The organization serves women who face barriers to economic participation, including those entering, reentering, or advancing in the workforce. Services are delivered locally through affiliates located in countries such as Australia, Canada, Guatemala, Italy, Jamaica, Luxembourg, Mexico, Mongolia, Netherlands, New Zealand, Portugal, Serbia, Singapore, Slovenia, the United Kingdom, and the United States (with locations across many states and cities). Each affiliate tailors support to the needs of women in its community.

## Programs and Services
Dress for Success offers several core programs:
- **Styling:** Volunteers help women select interview outfits and provide guidance and support for upcoming interviews.
- **Leadership:** Participants learn to initiate social change, becoming assets within their local Dress for Success organizations and broader communities.
- **Career Reskilling:** Training and resources to help women adapt to evolving job markets.
- **Entrepreneurship Support:** Assistance for women starting or growing their own businesses.
- **Sustainability and Health & Wellness:** Programs focused on environmental responsibility and personal well-being.

All programs are administered by local affiliates, while the global network provides overarching support, shared best practices, and connections among volunteers, corporate partners, and NGOs.

## How to Get Involved
Readers can contribute through time, talents, treasure, and needed goods. Specific opportunities and requirements may vary by location and are updated regularly on the organization’s website.

- **Time:** Volunteer at a local boutique, assist with styling sessions, support events, or mentor participants.
- **Talents:** Share professional expertise in areas such as career coaching, interview preparation, leadership development, or business advice.
- **Treasure:** Make a financial donation, contribute gift cards or store credit, or participate in matching gift programs to amplify impact.
- **Needed Goods:** Provide gift cards or store credit that enable the purchase of items not kept in standard inventory.

For the most current volunteer roles, donation options, and in‑kind giving guidelines, please visit the [Dress for Success official website](https://dressforsuccess.org/about-us/what-we-do/).

## Before You Give
Before contributing, review the latest program details, local affiliate availability, financial information, and volunteer requirements on the Dress for Success website to ensure your support aligns with current needs and operations.
