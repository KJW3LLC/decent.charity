**City Harvest: Rescuing Food for New York City Neighbors**
==================================================

City Harvest works to end hunger in New York City by rescuing surplus food and distributing it to neighbors in need. Through food rescue, distribution, education, and advocacy, the organization provides fresh, nutritious food at no cost to hundreds of community partners across the five boroughs.

## Organization Background
City Harvest began more than 40 years ago when a volunteer noticed edible food being discarded and imagined redirecting it to people experiencing hunger. That idea sparked the food rescue movement, and City Harvest has since grown into New York City’s largest food rescue organization. Its mission is to end hunger in communities throughout New York City by rescuing and delivering high‑quality food, offering nutrition and culinary education, and advocating for systemic change.

## Communities Served
City Harvest serves New Yorkers who face food insecurity, operating across all five boroughs. Food is delivered free of charge to a network of hundreds of food pantries, soup kitchens, Mobile Markets®, and Community Partner Distributions. The organization focuses on providing fresh produce, which makes up about 77% of the rescued food, to help neighbors thrive.

## Programs and Services
City Harvest’s core work includes:
- **Food Rescue:** collecting surplus food from more than 1,800 local and national donors and delivering nearly 274,000 pounds each day.
- **Food Distribution:** getting rescued food to partner agencies such as pantries, soup kitchens, Mobile Markets®, and Community Partner Distributions.
- **Nutrition & Culinary Education:** offering programs that teach healthy cooking and nutrition skills.
- **Disaster Response:** mobilizing resources to provide emergency food assistance when needed.
These activities are carried out locally in New York City, although the food rescued comes from both local and national sources.

- City Harvest has rescued and delivered over one billion pounds of food since its founding.  
- Each day, the organization rescues about 274,000 pounds of food, 77% of which is fresh produce.
