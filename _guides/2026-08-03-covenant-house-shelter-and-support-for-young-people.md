**Covenant House: Shelter and Support for Young People**
====================================================

Covenant House is a nonprofit organization that provides immediate and long‑term support to young people facing homelessness and survivors of trafficking across North and Central America. Since opening its doors in 1972, it has welcomed over 1.5 million youth, offering 24/7 shelter and a range of strengths‑based services designed to help them build independent lives. Learn more about its mission and work at the [Covenant House official website](https://www.covenanthouse.org/about-us).

## Organization Background
- Founded in 1972.  
- Described as the largest primarily privately funded charity in North and Central America serving youth experiencing homelessness and trafficking.  
- Mission: provide love, shelter, and hope; help youth transform their lives and achieve independence.  
- Operates 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.  
- Funded by individual donations, philanthropic institutions, and business partnerships.

## Communities Served
- Serves youth facing homelessness, including survivors of human trafficking.  
- Pays particular attention to LGBTQ+ youth, young people of color, and pregnant and parenting youth.  
- Works in cities throughout North and Central America.  
- Addresses related issues such as human trafficking, foster‑care gaps, income inequality, lack of affordable housing, racial discrimination, and mental and emotional health.

## Programs and Services
- Street and Community Outreach.  
- Crisis Care and Shelter (provides food, clothing, medical attention, and a safe place to sleep at no cost).  
- Long‑Term Support.  
- Education & Employment assistance.  
- Physical & Mental Health Support.  
- Affordable‑for‑Youth Housing.  
- Programs are strengths‑based, emphasizing sanctuary, structure, value communication, and choice to empower youth.  
- Services are delivered through local sites within the Covenant House network across the region.
