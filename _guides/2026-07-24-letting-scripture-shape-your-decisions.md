**Letting Scripture Shape Your Decisions**  
==================================================

When we face choices—big or small—it’s easy to rely on gut feelings, advice from friends, or the latest trend. Yet Scripture invites us to let God’s wisdom shape the very way we decide, grounding our steps in truth that leads to love, justice, and peace.

## Scripture Foundation  

Several passages remind us that God’s Word is a lamp for our path and a guide for our heart.  

> **Scripture Reflection:** *“Your word is a lamp for my feet, a light on my path.”* — Psalm 119:105 (NIV)  
> Scripture quotations are from the New International Version (NIV).  

This image of light shows that Scripture illuminates the next step, not just the destination. When we pause to let the Bible speak, we receive direction that aligns with God’s character.

Jesus summed up the whole law in two commands that shape every decision:  

> **Scripture Reflection:** *“Love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your mind.’ This is the first and greatest commandment. And the second is like it: ‘Love your neighbor as yourself.’”* — Matthew 22:37‑39 (NIV)  
> Scripture quotations are from the New International Version (NIV).  

When a choice is before us, we can ask: Does this option help me love God more fully? Does it show love to the people around me?  

The wisdom literature adds a practical angle:  

> **Scripture Reflection:** *“Trust in the LORD with all your heart and lean not on your own understanding; in all your ways submit to him, and he will make your paths straight.”* — Proverbs 3:5‑6 (NIV)  
> Scripture quotations are from the New International Version (NIV).  

Submitting our plans to God doesn’t mean we stop thinking; it means we invite His perspective into our thinking, allowing Him to straighten the road ahead.

## What This Looks Like in Daily Life  

- **Work decisions:** Before accepting a new project or job, we might read Psalm 119:105 and ask, “Does this opportunity let me use my gifts to serve others and honor God?”  
- **Relationships:** When navigating conflict, we recall Matthew 22:37‑39 and consider whether our response reflects love for God and the other person, even if it means apologizing or setting healthy boundaries.  
- **Finances:** Proverbs 3:5‑6 encourages us to bring our budgeting to prayer, seeking God’s guidance rather than relying solely on spreadsheets or societal pressure.  
- **Time management:** We can examine how we spend hours each week, asking whether our schedule reflects the priority of loving God (through worship, prayer, Scripture) and loving neighbor (through service, rest, and community).  

In each area, Scripture doesn’t give a detailed checklist; it offers a mindset that shapes our discernment.

## Questions for Reflection  

1. When faced with a recent decision, which Scripture came to mind, and how did it influence your thinking?  
2. Are there areas of your life where you tend to rely more on personal preference than on biblical principles? Why might that be?  
3. How does the command to love God and neighbor help you weigh competing options (e.g., career advancement vs. family time)?  
4. Recall a time when you felt God “made your paths straight” after you surrendered a plan to Him. What did you learn?  
5. What practical habit could you add to remind yourself to consult Scripture before making choices?  
6. How can discussing decisions with a trusted friend or small group help you hear God’s Word more clearly?  

## A Simple Practice This Week  

**Practice:** Choose one decision you’ll face in the next few days (e.g., a conversation, a purchase, a schedule change). Before acting, spend five minutes reading Psalm 119:105 and Proverbs 3:5‑6, then write a brief note: “How might God’s Word guide me here?”  

**Gentle next step:** Share your note with a friend, mentor, or small group and invite their perspective, asking them to point out any biblical truths you might have missed.  

## Prayer  

Gracious God, thank You for giving us Your Word as a light and a guide. Help us to pause, to listen, and to let Scripture shape the choices we make each day. May our decisions reflect love for You and love for those around us, leading us into the good plans You have for us. In Jesus’ name we pray, Amen.  

## Key Takeaways  

- Scripture illuminates our path, showing us the next faithful step.  
- Loving God and loving neighbor are the core criteria for every decision.  
- Trusting God with God’s guidance brings clarity, even when we don’t see the whole picture.  
- Simple habits—prayerful reading, brief reflection, and community conversation—let Scripture shape our choices.
