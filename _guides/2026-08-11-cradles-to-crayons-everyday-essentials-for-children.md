**Cradles to Crayons: Everyday Essentials for Children**
====================================================

Cradles to Crayons provides children from birth through age 12 with the everyday essentials they need to thrive at home, at school, and at play. Founded in Boston in 2002, the organization has grown to serve communities in Greater Philadelphia and Chicagoland while maintaining a vision of a future free of childhood poverty.

## Organization Background
The story began when founder Lynn Margherio noticed outgrown and never‑worn clothing while helping her niece get dressed. Less than two years later, in 2002, she launched Cradles to Crayons in Boston. Since then, the organization has expanded to Greater Philadelphia (2006) and Chicagoland (2016). Its mission is to give children the everyday essentials they need to thrive, and its overarching vision is a future free of childhood poverty. The model turns compassion into action by connecting community members who have resources with those who are in need, primarily through meaningful volunteer experiences for people of all ages and clear communication about the impact of their support.

## Communities Served
Cradles to Crayons serves children from birth through age 12 in the Boston, Greater Philadelphia, and Chicagoland areas. The focus is on children who lack everyday essentials such as clothing and other basic items needed for daily life. Services are delivered locally within each of these metropolitan regions.

## Programs and Services
The organization's principal work is collecting and distributing everyday essentials—including clothing—to children in need. These services are carried out locally through The Giving Factory and community‑based initiatives in each location. There is no national program; each site serves its surrounding community.

## How to Get Involved
- **Time**: Volunteer at The Giving Factory, in your community, or through the Giving Corps.  
- **Talents**: Contribute your skills and expertise to support volunteer experiences and impact communication.  
- **Treasure**: Provide financial support that helps fund the organization’s work.  
- **Needed Goods**: Donate new or gently used clothing and other essentials via a local drop‑off or by hosting a local drive.
