**Habitat for Humanity: Building Access to Safe, Affordable Homes**
====================================================================

Habitat for Humanity is a global nonprofit housing organization that works in local communities across all 50 U.S. states and approximately 70 countries. Its vision is a world where everyone has a decent place to live, and it pursues this by building and repairing homes in partnership with families and volunteers. The organization emphasizes that safe, affordable shelter leads to improvements in health, education, economic opportunity and community well-being.

## Organization Background
Habitat for Humanity was founded in 1976 by Millard and Linda Fuller. As a global nonprofit housing organization, it operates through a network of local affiliates that partner with families to build, rehabilitate, and preserve homes. The organization’s mission is to create innovative housing solutions, and it seeks to achieve a world where everyone has a decent place to live. Families who partner with Habitat build their own homes alongside volunteers and pay an affordable mortgage. Habitat also advocates for policy changes to improve access to adequate affordable housing.

## Communities Served
Habitat serves households in need of safe, affordable housing in the United States and abroad. Its work reaches local communities in all 50 states and about 70 countries, addressing needs such as new home construction, repairs for older adults, neighborhood revitalization, and disaster recovery. The organization focuses on families seeking decent shelter, older adults requiring home modifications, and communities recovering from natural disasters, without making broad claims about any specific demographic beyond those needs described in its public materials.

## Programs and Services
Habitat’s principal work includes home construction, older adults’ home repair, revitalizing communities, disaster response, and innovation in housing solutions. These services are carried out by local affiliates, while certain volunteer and partnership programs operate on a national or international scale. Examples of volunteer‑focused initiatives mentioned on the site are Women Build, Home Preservation, Veterans Build, Youth Programs, Travel & build Global Village, RV Care‑A‑Vanners, Collegiate Challenge, and various build events. Partnership categories include business partners, faith partners, community organizations, and corporate giving programs. Advocacy efforts aim to influence policies that affect housing affordability. All programs are administered through the local affiliate structure, with national coordination for initiatives such as AmeriCorps and disaster response.

## How to Get Involved
Individuals can contribute through time, talents, treasure, and needed goods. For the most current opportunities and requirements, please visit the Habitat for Humanity website: [Habitat for Humanity official website](https://www.habitat.org/about).

**Time** – Volunteer on local build sites, join Women Build, Veterans Build, Youth Programs, Global Village trips, RV Care‑A‑Vanners, Collegiate Challenge, or participate in build events and advocacy activities.

**Talents** – Offer professional skills such as construction, repair, planning, project management, or services through business, faith, or community partnerships.

**Treasure** – Make financial contributions including one‑time donations, monthly giving, honor or memorial gifts, gift catalog purchases, stock donations, donor‑advised funds, planned giving, workplace giving, corporate giving, or gifts‑in‑kind partnerships.

**Needed Goods** – Donate building materials, vehicles, or other items to Habitat ReStores, or contribute goods and materials directly to local affiliates.
