**Meals on Wheels America: Nutrition and Connection for Older Adults**
============================================================

Meals on Wheels America is a national leadership organization that supports a network of local providers delivering nutritious meals and social connection to older adults across the United States. Its mission is to empower community programs to improve seniors’ health and quality of life so that no one faces hunger or isolation.

## Organization Background
Meals on Wheels America has been operating for more than 50 years, working to strengthen local Meals on Wheels programs nationwide. As a national organization, it does not deliver meals directly but provides support, resources, and advocacy to more than 5,000 independent community‑based providers that serve seniors in their neighborhoods. Its stated vision is an America where all seniors live nourished lives with independence and dignity.

## Communities Served
The organization’s network serves older adults who experience hunger, isolation, or related challenges that affect health and well‑being. Services are intended for seniors living in communities across the country, with a focus on those who are homebound or otherwise unable to access regular nutrition and social support. The source notes that the network serves over 2 million seniors each year, while many more remain on waitlists for assistance.

## Programs and Services
Meals on Wheels America’s work centers on three national‑level focus areas that aid local providers:
- **Programming:** expanding reach, increasing effectiveness, and providing funding to grow services.
- **Partnerships:** building collaborations to increase support for communities with greatest need.
- **Influence:** advocating for policies and funding, raising public awareness, and supporting research that promotes senior health and connection.

Direct meal delivery, social visits, and related assistance are carried out by the local affiliates; Meals on Wheels America’s role is to strengthen those efforts through training, grants, advocacy, and strategic guidance.
