**Mercy Housing: Affordable Homes with Resident Support**
==================================================

Mercy Housing is a national nonprofit organization dedicated to creating stable, vibrant, and healthy communities through affordable, program‑enriched housing. It serves families, seniors, and people with special needs who lack the economic resources to access quality housing, aiming to help residents develop their full potential.

## Organization Background
Mercy Housing describes itself as one of the nation’s largest affordable housing organizations. Its mission is to create stable, vibrant, and healthy communities by developing, financing, and operating affordable, program‑enriched housing for families, seniors, and people with special needs who lack the economic resources to access quality housing opportunities. The organization participates in the development, preservation, management, and/or financing of such housing across the United States. It acquires and renovates existing properties and develops new affordable rental units. Mercy Housing’s core values are respect, justice, and mercy.

## Communities Served
Mercy Housing serves families, seniors, and people with special needs, as well as low‑ and moderate‑income populations throughout the United States. The organization operates nationally, with regional offices in California, Lakefront, Mountain Plains, Northwest, and Southeast. Its housing is intended for individuals who cannot afford market‑rate housing due to limited economic resources.

## Programs and Services
- **Affordable Housing:** Mercy Housing develops, preserves, manages, and finances affordable, program‑enriched housing for low‑ and moderate‑income households. This includes acquiring and renovating existing buildings and constructing new rental properties.  
- **Resident Services:** To support residents in living to their full potential, the organization provides services in health and wellness, after‑school programs, financial stability, community involvement, and housing stability. Resident Services are currently available at approximately 75 % of Mercy Housing properties, with a goal to extend them to every community.  
- **Mercy Community Capital:** An affiliate of Mercy Housing, Mercy Community Capital offers loans and support to communities and infrastructure projects that create and sustain affordable housing. It collaborates with socially‑responsible developers to finance single‑ and multifamily homes for rental and homeownership.

All of these programs are part of Mercy Housing’s national network, while the delivery of housing and resident services occurs at individual properties across the country.
