**The Undies Project: Meeting a Basic Need With Dignity**  
====================================================

The Undies Project provides new underwear to people experiencing homelessness, living in shelters, or facing low‑income challenges. By addressing this often‑overlooked need, the organization aims to improve hygiene, comfort, and dignity for individuals and families.

## Organization Background  
The Undies Project Inc. was founded in 2015 by Lucy Langley and Laura Delaflor after volunteering at Neighbor to Neighbor in Connecticut. It became a registered 501(c)(3) public charity in 2016. The mission is to donate new underwear to men, women, and children in need while educating communities about the unmet demand for this essential item. The organization relies on donations of money and new underwear from local communities, individuals, and businesses, and also fundraises to purchase underwear at wholesale prices for distribution.

## Communities Served  
The Undies Project serves people who are homeless, residing in shelters, or living on low incomes. It works through partner organizations that directly support these populations. The organization’s headquarters and mailing address are located at The Undies Project Inc., c/o Diamond Hill United Methodist Church, 521 E. Putnam Avenue, Cos Cob, CT 06807.

## Programs and Services  
The core program involves collecting donations of new underwear and monetary contributions, then distributing the underwear via partner agencies that serve homeless individuals, shelter residents, and low‑income families. In addition to direct donations, the organization fundraises to purchase underwear at wholesale prices to expand its reach. Educational outreach about the importance of clean underwear is also part of its work.
