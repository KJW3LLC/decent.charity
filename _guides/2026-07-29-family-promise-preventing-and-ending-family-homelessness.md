**Family Promise: Preventing and Ending Family Homelessness**
============================================================

Family Promise is a national nonprofit dedicated to preventing and ending family homelessness. Through a network of local affiliates, it provides emergency shelter, prevention services, stabilization support, and housing assistance, along with community resources such as food pantries and clothing closets. The organization emphasizes a community‑based response that helps families with children achieve sustainable independence.

## Organization Background
Family Promise operates as a nationwide organization with a central office that oversees a network of local affiliates. Its mission is to prevent and end family homelessness and to help families achieve sustainable independence through a community‑based approach. The organization has earned nine consecutive 4‑star ratings from Charity Navigator, reflecting strong financial health and accountability.

## Communities Served
Family Promise serves families with children who are at risk of or experiencing homelessness. Services are delivered locally by affiliates in communities across the United States, addressing needs such as emergency shelter, housing stability, food, clothing, and transportation assistance.

## Programs and Services
Family Promise’s work centers on four core areas: homelessness prevention and shelter diversion, emergency shelter, stabilization, and housing. In addition, affiliates offer community services that may include food pantries, drop‑in community centers, clothing closets, and transportation programs. While the national office provides oversight, training, and resources, all direct services are carried out by local affiliates.
