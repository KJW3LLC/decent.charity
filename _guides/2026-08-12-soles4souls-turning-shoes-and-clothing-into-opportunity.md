**Soles4Souls: Turning Shoes and Clothing Into Opportunity**  
====================================================

Soles4Souls exists to turn surplus shoes and clothing into opportunity for people and the planet. By collecting usable footwear and apparel, the organization helps individuals access education, work, and greater mobility while reducing waste.  

## Organization Background  
Soles4Souls describes its mission as ending wasted potential by turning surplus into opportunity. The organization focuses on shoes and clothing as tools that support education, work, and life for people living in poverty. It operates as a registered 501(c)3 nonprofit, with activities spanning the USA, Canada, Europe, the UK, and Asia.  

## Communities Served  
Soles4Souls serves people facing homelessness, those affected by natural disasters, and individuals experiencing economic hardship through its 4Relief program. It also supports children experiencing homelessness who need athletic shoes to attend school via the 4EveryKid program. Through 4Opportunity, gently used shoes and clothing are provided to vetted wholesalers in developing nations, enabling local entrepreneurs to build small businesses that generate sustainable income for families.  

## Programs and Services  
The organization’s principal work is organized around three core programs:  

- **4Relief**: Distributes new shoes and clothing free of charge to people confronting homelessness, natural disasters, and economic hardship.  
- **4Opportunity**: Supplies gently used shoes and clothing to vetted wholesalers in developing nations, who then sell the items at low, fair prices to local entrepreneurs. These micro‑enterprises create jobs and income for families.  
- **4EveryKid**: Provides brand‑new athletic shoes to children experiencing homelessness so they can attend school with confidence and participate fully in school activities.  

These programs are carried out through a network of partners and affiliates across the organization’s regional locations.
