**The Wardrobe: Addressing Clothing Insecurity for Work and Life**
================================================================================

The Wardrobe provides clothing and support to people facing transitions where the cost of apparel can be a barrier to employment, independence, or stability. Operating in Southeastern Pennsylvania, the organization blends free services with a resale store to fund its mission and serve a diverse community.

## Organization Background
Founded in 1995 as The Working Wardrobe, the group originally helped low‑income women obtain interview suits and support to enter the workforce. Over three decades, the mission expanded to address a broader range of life transitions and evolving ideas of work and career. In 2020, after 25 years of partnership, Wings for Success merged with The Wardrobe, adding two new sites in Exton and Kennett Square, PA. The organization now marks its 30th anniversary in 2025 and operates seven Wardrobe locations across multiple counties in Southeastern PA.

## Communities Served
The Wardrobe serves people of all gender identities and backgrounds who are seeking employment, moving toward independence after incarceration, entering recovery, or transitioning into adulthood. Services are available to anyone in need, with free clothing provided to those who qualify through referrals from more than 150 partner organizations. The resale store is open to the public, offering clothing priced to match any budget.

## Programs and Services
The Wardrobe’s core work includes:
- Free clothing appointments for individuals referred by partner agencies.
- A public resale store whose sales help fund programs.
- Acceptance of clothing and toiletries donations, as well as upcycled garments for resale.
- Partnerships with community organizations to expand reach.
Services are local to Southeastern Pennsylvania; there is no national network described in the source.
