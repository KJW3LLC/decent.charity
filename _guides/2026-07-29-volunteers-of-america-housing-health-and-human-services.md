**Volunteers of America: Housing, Health and Human Services**
====================================================

Volunteers of America is a national nonprofit organization that provides a broad range of housing, health, and human services to individuals and families across the United States. Through a network of local affiliates, the organization delivers programs that address homelessness, behavioral health needs, disability support, aging services, and veteran assistance. Learn more about their services on the [Volunteers of America official website](https://www.voa.org/our-services/).

## Organization Background
Volunteers of America operates as a nationwide federation of locally governed affiliates that share a common brand and mission. The organization notes a history spanning over 130 years, reflected in its “130 Years of Resilience” initiative. Its work focuses on delivering housing, health, and supportive services to people experiencing a variety of life challenges, using a model in which national leadership provides resources and oversight while affiliates implement programs tailored to their communities.

## Communities Served
The organization serves diverse populations, including:
- Children, youths, and families needing child care, family support, early education, and adoption services  
- Adults facing behavioral health challenges, substance use disorders, or housing instability  
- Older adults requiring health care, home assistance, assisted living, independent living, or meals programs  
- Veterans seeking behavioral health support, housing assistance, and homelessness prevention  
- Persons with disabilities, including those with developmental or intellectual disabilities  
- Women and families accessing services similar to those for children and youths  

Affiliates are located in numerous states, such as Alaska, Chesapeake & Carolinas, Colorado, Dakotas, Delaware Valley, Florida, Greater New York, Illinois, Los Angeles, Massachusetts, Michigan, Minnesota and Wisconsin, North Louisiana, Northern California & Northern Nevada, Northern New England, Northern Rockies, Ohio & Indiana, Oklahoma, Oregon, Pennsylvania, Puerto Rico, South Central Louisiana, Southeast (AL, GA, MS), Southeast Louisiana, Southwest Texas, Upstate New York, Utah, and Western Washington.

## Programs and Services
Volunteers of America’s principal work spans several service areas, most of which are administered locally by affiliates with national support:

- **Behavioral and Mental Health**: addiction and substance use disorder treatment, family‑focused treatment, mental health crisis counseling, and residential mental health housing.  
- **Housing and Homelessness Assistance**: affordable housing, emergency shelters, transitional housing, permanent supportive housing, rapid rehousing, and homelessness prevention programs.  
- **Child and Family Services**: child care resource and referral, childcare, family support, early educational programs, Operation Backpack®, and adoption services.  
- **Older Adult Services**: comprehensive home health, life plan communities, Community Home Health Agency (HHA), Long‑Term Care Program of All‑Inclusive Care for the Elderly (PACE), short‑term rehabilitation, skilled nursing/memory care, assisted living, independent living, and meals programs.  
- **Veteran Services**: behavioral health, addiction treatment, mental health housing/residential, housing and homelessness prevention, permanent supportive housing, rapid rehousing, shelters, transitional housing, and supportive services for veterans and their families.  
- **Disability Services**: developmental disability services, intellectual disability services, and related housing and support.  
- **Community Outreach and Investment**: thrift stores, food pantries and food assistance, and community investment programs.  

All services are delivered through local affiliates; the national organization provides branding, training, and resource coordination.

## How to Get Involved
Individuals can support Volunteers of America in several ways. For the most current opportunities and requirements, please refer to the [Volunteers of America official website](https://www.voa.org/our-services/).

- **Time**: Volunteer opportunities vary by affiliate and may include direct service, event support, or administrative help.  
- **Talents**: Share professional skills such as counseling, legal aid, teaching, or trade expertise with local programs.  
- **Treasure**: Make financial contributions to sustain programs; donation options are outlined on the website.  
- **Needed Goods**: Donate items such as clothing, household goods, or non‑perishable food to support thrift stores and food pantries.
